WHY THE REBUILD?
================================================================================

The OpenCV.js (WebAssembly) approach had persistent initialization issues
that couldn't be reliably fixed. The new Python backend:

✅ Works reliably on Mac M4
✅ Faster (native vs WASM)
✅ Same algorithms (ORB + AGNES)
✅ Better error messages
✅ Real-time progress updates
✅ Easier to maintain
